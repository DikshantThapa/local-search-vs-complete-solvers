## 1. Why (referee gaps, REFEREE_JHEUR.md section 1 point 2 and 3, section 4)
(a) SAT baseline was 1994 WalkSAT/SKC, not modern SLS; (b) only the local search was tuned, CP-SAT ran defaults; (c) Latin-square losses came from one plain min-conflicts LS; (d) only random families, k=3 only, no instances not generated by us. v3d adds a modern SLS, a tuned CP-SAT arm with the same documented effort class as ls_nb, a tabu LS, k=5, and public benchmark files.

## 2. Solvers added
- `probsat_nb` (numba): probSAT (Balint and Schoening 2012), break-only score, pick a random unsatisfied clause, flip variable v with probability f(break(v)) / sum over the clause. k<=3: polynomial f = (eps + b)^(-cb), cb=2.06, eps=0.9. k>=4: exponential f = cb^(-b), cb=3.7. Constants are the published values as given in the brief; the paper was not re-opened (UNVERIFIED by me, check before citing). No restarts, no tuning, no make-score. Initial assignment random.
- `ls_tabu_nb` (numba, qwh and qcp): same representation and cost as ls_nb (rows are permutations, cost = column duplicates). Each step evaluates ALL swaps of a conflicted hole cell with another hole of its row and takes the best (random ties); a swap is tabu if it would restore a value to a cell it left less than tenure + rnd(tenure/2) steps ago, unless it gives cost below the restart's best (aspiration). Restart (fresh random rows) after `stall * N` non-improving steps. tenure and stall tuned (section 4).
- `cpsat_tuned`: cpsat_strong model (1 worker) plus one parameter text per family chosen by tune_cpsat.py from `CP_VARIANTS`: default, linearization_level 0 / 2, search_branching PORTFOLIO_SEARCH / FIXED_SEARCH, symmetry_level 0 / 2, cp_model_presolve false. Variants not accepted by the installed OR-tools are dropped and logged. Outside-benchmark sets use the ksat choice.
- Unchanged: `ls_nb` (also on ksat, WalkSAT/SKC), `cpsat_strong`, `cadical195`.

## 4. Tuning (run BEFORE the stage; `tune_cpsat.py`; tuning seeds 2000-2002 only)
Same effort class on both arms, stated honestly: ls_nb noise tuning in v3 was 6 settings x 3 runs x 2-3 s on 4 cells (about 60 runs); CP-SAT tuning here is 8 variants x 3 seeds x 3 cells x 5 families = 360 runs at 5 s (more effort than ls_nb got). Tabu: 4 tenures x 2 stalls x 3 seeds x 4 cells = 96 runs at 5 s. Rule: winner = most solved over the 9 runs of a family, tie lower PAR2 (2T per failure), tie default; a non-default must beat default by >= 1 solved or >= 10% PAR2 else default stays. probsat constants, ls_nb constants and cpsat_strong are not re-tuned. Results are written to tuned_cpsat.json and tuned_ls.json and copied into the env JSON; a null result (default wins everywhere) is reported as such. Tuning cells are inside the stage grid but tuning seeds are not stage seeds.

## 5. Hypotheses (D1 PRIMARY; D2-D6 exploratory). Definitions: solved = verified SAT within T; decided = solved or UNSAT proof within T. Intervals: Wilson 95% for single proportions; paired difference = percentile bootstrap over instances, 10,000 resamples, numpy seed 0; paired exact one-sided McNemar p for exploratory directions. Cells with < 16 completed instances in a solver are excluded (-> "not evaluable"). Holm over the exploratory list's McNemar p-values (D2, D3, D4; D5 and D6 are descriptive without p). Thresholds are effect-size choices made here, not derived: 0.5 and 3/4 as in H1/C1; 0.10 for D2 (one instance in ten, the smallest effect worth a sentence at n=20: 2 instances); 0.20 for D3 and D4 (4 of 20 instances).
- **D1 (PRIMARY).** bnq n=200, p in {.84,.86,.88,.90}: paired bootstrap 95% lower bound of solved(ls_nb) - solved(cpsat_tuned) >= 0.5 in at least 3 of 4 cells. Supported if so; falsified if two or more cells fail; vs cpsat_strong shown as information.
- **D2 (direction fixed before data).** ksat n=350, r in {4.2, 4.26, 4.3} (near threshold 4.26): solved(probsat_nb) - solved(cadical195) >= +0.10 in at least 2 of 3 cells. Expected direction: probSAT solves more SAT-side instances in 10 s at this size. Falsified if CDCL is not behind by 0.10 in 2 cells (that includes a tie or CDCL ahead). Info: ls_nb (WalkSAT) fraction.
- **D3.** ksat5 n=200, r in {20, 20.5, 21}: solved(probsat_nb) - max(solved(cadical195), solved(cpsat_tuned)) >= +0.20 in at least 2 of 3 cells (best rival chosen per cell by solved fraction).
- **D4.** qwh N=40, h in {.35, .40}: solved(best complete of cpsat_strong, cpsat_tuned, cadical195) - solved(best of ls_nb, ls_tabu_nb) >= +0.20 in BOTH cells ("the Latin loss survives a stronger and a second local search"). Falsified otherwise. qcp N=40 f=.38,.42,.46 reported descriptively only.
- **D5.** Outside benchmarks: supported if probsat_nb solves >= 0.90 of the 20 uf250 files and >= 0.80 of the 20 flat150 files within 10 s; QG descriptive (22 instances, no directional claim). Wilson intervals reported for every set and solver. Exploratory, 20 files per set is a small sample.
- **D6.** Sensitivity: for every cell with both CP-SAT arms, Kendall tau-b between the solved fractions of {non-CP-SAT solvers + cpsat_strong} and {same + cpsat_tuned}; supported if the mean over cells where tau is defined is >= 0.80 ("ranking unchanged between default and tuned CP-SAT"). Cells with a constant vector have undefined tau and are dropped, their number is reported. Descriptive.
All verdicts reported, none dropped. Cell labels (SAT-side etc.) are not used in v3d tests; cells are named in advance above.

## 6. Analysis (`analyze_v3d.py CONFIRM.csv --ext EXT.csv`)
Asserts no late answer remains; stops if one instance receives both SAT and UNSAT; writes cells.csv (Wilson, decided, PAR10, median time of solved) and hypotheses.md. Self-test on a fake CSV: `python analyze_v3d.py --selftest`. ECDF figures are not generated (no plotting dependency); cells.csv suffices to plot.
